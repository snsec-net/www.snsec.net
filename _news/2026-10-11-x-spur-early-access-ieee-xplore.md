[Jisoo Kim](/people/#jisoo-kim)’s paper, “X-SPUR: Explainable Surprisal-Based Protocol-Aware Unsupervised Reasoning for Automotive Ethernet Intrusion Detection,” is now [available online](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11732073) in the Early Access area of IEEE Xplore. The article has been accepted for inclusion in a future issue of *IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics (TII)* and can be cited by its DOI, [10.1109/TII.2026.3736030](https://doi.org/10.1109/TII.2026.3736030).
