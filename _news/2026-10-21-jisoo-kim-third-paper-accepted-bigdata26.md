[Jisoo Kim](/people/#jisoo-kim) has had her [third paper](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6aItteMAAAAJ), "Beyond Bot Labels: Retrospective Consequence-Grounded Ranking of Attributed Cache Harm in E-Commerce Logs," accepted as a regular paper at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Big Data (IEEE BigData 2026), a conference with an acceptance rate of 19.4%. Rather than labeling e-commerce sessions as bots or humans, the paper ranks them by the cache harm they actually cause real shoppers, charging each shopper's cache miss to the session that evicted the object and learning to predict this harm from session behavior alone.
